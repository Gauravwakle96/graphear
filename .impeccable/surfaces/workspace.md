visual step has a text control; review, editing, comparison and learning share one graph.

repair a connection and inspect the consequences. Sources remain visible during review.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact left navigation, top brand/actions, diagram title and view
controls, broad graph canvas on the left, selected-step exploration panel on the right.
FORM: Accepted canvas-and-explorer composition; functional path highlighting is the
signature interaction. seed:8ed2b4b6e44a610e. User-pinned green material overrides catalog palettes.
